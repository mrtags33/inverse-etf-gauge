How much of the leveraged-ETF money is betting on a fall?

Inverse ETFs' share of leveraged-plus-inverse ETF dollar volume. A high share means traders are paying for downside bets in a hurry, which has tended to happen near bottoms. Warren Pies has cited about 40% for a tactical low, 50% before calling a bottom in a sharp sell-off, and 60% at major bottoms.

How it's calculated

Dollar volume is each fund's closing price × shares traded. The N-day reading adds up N trading days of inverse and leveraged dollar volume before dividing, so heavy days count for more. Percentiles rank each reading against the same window's previous 756 trading days (about 3 years).
